# ESP LIVE Compliance Training and Readiness Evidence

This Chat 5 subsystem supports Elevate Souls Productions Creator Network governance inside the Elevate Souls Productions Content Creation Command Center — Powered by Aura AI.

## Purpose

The feature gives active ESP Creators and Agents a versioned policy pack, a 40-question knowledge assessment, and an auditable readiness view. It also lets an owner-authorized reviewer record bounded manual verification evidence for prerequisites that the website cannot independently prove.

It is deliberately an **evidence and education layer**, not a TikTok authorization system and not legal certification.

## Current source basis

The 27 August 2026 policy pack is grounded in current TikTok Community Guidelines and LIVE guidance, including these requirements and risk areas:

- LIVE creators must maintain a safe environment and are responsible for activity in their room.
- LIVE creators are responsible for third-party tools they enable, including voice-to-text, real-time translation and on-screen comment displays.
- TikTok states users must be 18 or older to go LIVE and to use monetization features covered by the current guidance.
- Commercial LIVE content must use the applicable disclosure controls.
- Hate, harassment, violence, child-safety harms, deceptive behavior, regulated activity and manipulative Gift behavior are safety/compliance concerns.
- Authenticity, rights-cleared media and intellectual-property rules remain applicable to LIVE content.

Primary TikTok source:

- https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features
- https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/

The wider Command Center compliance registry remains authoritative for privacy, AI transparency, child protection, accessibility, intellectual-property and professional-advice boundaries.

## Role boundary

The feature reads existing ESP membership state but never writes it. Passing the assessment, acknowledging policies or receiving an owner verification:

- does not grant Creator access;
- does not grant Agent access;
- does not change mentor/admin/owner authority;
- does not activate or alter a paid membership;
- does not modify billing or credits;
- does not call TikTok or alter a TikTok account.

Only accounts already holding an active ESP `creator`, `agent` or `both` role can use member training routes.

## 40-question assessment

The assessment covers:

1. LIVE host responsibility and third-party tools.
2. Age, account standing and feature eligibility.
3. Hate, bullying, harassment, threats and dangerous conduct.
4. Self-harm, sexual safety, child safeguarding and vulnerable people.
5. Gifts, manipulation, gambling and commercial disclosure.
6. Authenticity, copyright/IP and AI transparency.
7. Privacy, location safety and personal data.
8. Medical/legal/financial professional boundaries.
9. ESP professional conduct, escalation and role separation.

A passing result requires at least 90 percent and all safety-critical questions correct. Attempts are append-only evidence. A pass is not represented as TikTok eligibility.

## Policy acknowledgements

All policy acknowledgements are versioned and append-only. A later decline or withdrawal creates a new record instead of rewriting history. Readiness requires a current acknowledgement for every policy in the current pack.

## Owner verification

The application cannot prove age, TikTok account standing, account uniqueness or Creator Network exclusivity merely from a browser session. The system therefore fails closed and allows an existing owner-authorized reviewer to record only an opaque evidence reference and a `verified` or `rejected` state.

The website does not store passports, driving licences, raw identity images or TikTok credentials in this workflow. Evidence references reject URLs and filesystem paths.

Required checks depend on ESP role:

- Creator / Both: age 18+, one LIVE account, TikTok account good standing, Creator Network exclusivity.
- Agent: age 18+, TikTok account good standing.

These checks remain manual governance evidence until an approved, documented provider/API can prove them safely.

## Readiness meaning

`ready_for_esp_compliance_review=true` means only that the current policy acknowledgements, current assessment and required owner evidence are complete in this subsystem.

It explicitly does **not** mean:

- TikTok LIVE has been enabled;
- TikTok has approved monetization;
- TikTok has confirmed Creator Network eligibility;
- every jurisdictional legal requirement has been satisfied;
- ESP role approval has been granted automatically.

Current platform rules and applicable law must still be checked at the point of operation. Where a rule is uncertain, the system should fail closed and route the matter to appropriate owner/platform/legal review.
