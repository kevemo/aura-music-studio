# Video Generation Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 1 — generation foundation

- Provider-neutral request/result contract.
- Real local/self-hosted renderer adapter.
- OpenAI video adapter.
- Runway text/image/video adapter.
- Persistent job/provenance model.
- Music-video storyboard planner.
- FFmpeg audio visualizer renderer.
- API capability contract.

## Phase 2 — studio integration

- Register authenticated video routes in the main application.
- Enforce Free/Base/Pro video entitlements and provider-cost quotas.
- Add per-project Video Studio UI.
- Upload/reference asset picker with tenant isolation.
- Job status polling and completed asset retrieval.
- Video history, retries, variants and deletion.

## Phase 3 — Aura music-video director

- Read Project DNA and lyric timing automatically.
- Beat/section-aware storyboard generation.
- Character/style continuity references across shots.
- Candidate-shot generation and quality scoring.
- Multi-shot assembly against the final master.
- Automatic captions/lyrics, title cards and credits.

## Phase 4 — social content factory

- TikTok/Reels/Shorts 9:16 presets.
- Hook extraction from the strongest song section.
- Multiple promotional cuts from one finished master.
- Clean, captioned and lyric variants.
- Cover-art motion teasers.
- Optional CTA/outro templates.

## Phase 5 — professional finishing

- Video-to-video restyling and extension.
- Upscaling where a configured provider supports it.
- Color/contrast finishing.
- Audio replacement from the studio final master.
- 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 reframing from the same edit.
- MP4 delivery and optional high-quality mezzanine exports.

No phase may represent metadata, a still, a storyboard, or a placeholder as a finished generated video.
